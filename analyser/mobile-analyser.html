Mobile Analyser

Paste any phone's IMEI number and instantly see which model it is — with its full specifications, photo and price in Pakistan.

Dial *#06# on any phone to see its IMEI. Dual-SIM phones have two IMEIs — both belong to the same model. Private by design: the analysis runs inside your own browser. Your IMEI is never sent to or stored on any server.

Pakistan — PTA approval / blocked status: whether a phone is PTA-approved, tax-paid or blocked is maintained only by the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority. To check, SMS your IMEI to 8484, or use the official PTA DIRBS portal (dirbs.pta.gov.pk) or the DIRBS mobile app. No website can read PTA's register — always confirm there before buying a used phone.

How the Mobile Analyser works

Every IMEI is 15 digits. The first 8 digits (the TAC) are allotted to one exact phone model, the next 6 are the device's own serial number, and the last digit is a check digit calculated from the other digits, which confirms the number is correctly formed. Our analyser verifies the check digit, reads the TAC against a database of more than 269,000 allotted codes covering 31,000+ models, and shows the phone's full specifications — plus its photo and current Pakistan price for models in our catalogue. Please note: a correct check digit proves only that the IMEI format is valid — it cannot prove that a phone is genuine or original, because a copied or reprogrammed IMEI passes the same check.
