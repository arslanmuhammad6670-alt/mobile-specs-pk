Best Gaming Phones in Pakistan (2026): Flagship Power From Rs 189,999

Published 2026-11-13 · Check Specs

Ask a shopkeeper for the best gaming phone and you will be shown whatever carries the highest margin that week. Our gaming guide works to a stricter rule: the processor. Every phone in it runs a flagship-grade chip — a Snapdragon 8 series, a Dimensity 9 series, an Apple A17 or newer, or the Exynos 2400 — because those are the processors that hold high frame rates in demanding games instead of merely loading them. Where the value sits

The Infinix GT 50 Pro undercuts the next phone in the guide by Rs 90,000. Its Dimensity 8400 Ultimate is a step below the true flagship chips — the weakest processor here — yet it is paired with a 144Hz AMOLED screen, up to 6,500 mAh of battery and bypass charging, which feeds power straight to the phone during plugged-in play so the battery rests. For PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty: Mobile at high settings, that is the value end of this list, and it is not especially close.

The Xiaomi 17T Pro is the specification sheet that stands out under Rs 300,000: a Dimensity 9500, a 144Hz display, the biggest battery in the guide at 7,000 mAh and 100W charging. The vivo X300 FE runs it close — Snapdragon 8 Gen 5, 6,500 mAh, 90W charging — and costs Rs 10,000 less.

The top end

The Galaxy S26 Ultra (Rs 449,999, Official) is the most complete Android phone in the guide, and the iPhone 18 Pro Max (Rs 645,000, Official) answers with the A20 Pro and the largest battery fitted to any iPhone in this list. One caution on the standard Galaxy S26 and S26+: their specification pages list an Exynos 2600 variant for international markets alongside the Snapdragon chip, so check which processor the unit in front of you actually carries before paying flagship money.

Three phones to question before buying

The iPhone 17e (Rs 289,000, Official) has a genuinely fast A19 chip, but its display specification lists no high refresh rate — for essentially the same money, the Xiaomi 17T Pro gives you 144Hz. The iPhone 17 Air (Rs 428,500, Official) has the A19 Pro and a 120Hz screen, yet its 3,149 mAh battery is by far the smallest here; a charger will feature in any long session. And the Galaxy S25 Ultra remains quick, but its market price of Rs 459,999 sits above the newer S26 Ultra's official Rs 449,999 — unless a shop discounts it properly, the newer phone is the better purchase.

Who should buy — and who should not

Competitive players on a limited budget: the GT 50 Pro. Players who game for hours away from a socket: the 17T Pro, or the Xiaomi 17 and its 6,330 mAh cell. iOS players: the iPhone 18 Pro over the Pro Max unless the larger screen matters to you, because the processor is identical. If your gaming stops at Ludo, Candy Crush and the odd round of 8 Ball Pool, nothing on this page is necessary — every phone here is built for far heavier work, and phones a third of the price will serve you just as well.

Frequently asked questions

What matters most in a gaming phone?

The processor first, because it sets the frame-rate ceiling a game can reach. Refresh rate comes second — a 120Hz or 144Hz screen only shows an advantage the chip can actually feed — and battery capacity plus charging speed decide how long sessions last in practice.

What is bypass charging?

A feature on the Infinix GT 50 Pro in this list: while you play plugged in, power runs the phone directly instead of cycling through the battery. The battery rests and the phone heats up less during long sessions, which is when heat does the most damage to sustained performance.

Why does this list start at Rs 189,999?

Because the guide only admits flagship-grade processors. No cheaper phone currently pairs one of those chips with a Pakistan retail price in our listings — below this point, you are choosing which compromise to accept rather than getting the full package.

Is the iPhone 17e a good gaming phone?

Half of one. The A19 processor is flagship-grade, but the display specification lists no high refresh rate, so motion will not look as smooth as on the 120Hz and 144Hz rivals at the same Rs 289,000.
