Best Phones Under Rs 50,000 in Pakistan (2026) — Top 6 Picks

Published 2026-11-12 · Check Specs

Rs 50,000 is the most competitive budget in Pakistan's phone market. At this price you no longer have to choose between a good screen, a capable camera and a big battery — the best models now offer two or three of those at once. This guide compares six phones we track on Check Specs, all on sale in Pakistan under Rs 50,000, and explains which one suits which kind of buyer.

1. Tecno Spark 30 Pro — best display in this budget (Rs 41,499)

The Spark 30 Pro is the cheapest phone here, yet it has the screen many pricier phones would envy: an AMOLED panel with a 120Hz refresh rate and up to 1700 nits peak brightness, so it stays readable in sunlight. The Helio G100 handles everyday apps and light gaming smoothly, the 108 MP main camera is sharp in daylight, and 33W charging fills the 5000 mAh battery at a sensible pace. If display quality matters most to you, this is the value pick. See full specs and price.

2. Infinix Hot 40 Pro — the gaming pick (Rs 44,999)

Infinix built the Hot 40 Pro around the Helio G99, a proven budget gaming chipset, paired with a 120Hz display and up to 12 GB of RAM. The 108 MP main camera and a 32 MP selfie camera with its own flash make it a strong all-rounder for its price, and 33W charging reaches about 75% in 35 minutes. Gamers on a budget should start here. See full specs and price.

3. Redmi 13 — the camera pick (Rs 44,999)

The Redmi 13's headline is its 108 MP main camera with PDAF autofocus, unusual at this price. You also get a 90Hz display, the Helio G91 Ultra chipset and a 5030 mAh battery with 33W charging. Storage runs to 256 GB, expandable by microSD. For buyers who photograph documents, products or family events all day, it is the safest camera choice under Rs 50,000. See full specs and price.

4. Vivo Y28 — the battery pick (Rs 46,999)

If you are away from a charger all day — riders, field staff, shopkeepers — the Y28's 6000 mAh battery is the biggest in this list, and its 44W charging is also the joint fastest here. The rest is sensible rather than flashy: a 90Hz display, Helio G85 chipset and a 50 MP main camera. It trades screen sharpness for endurance, and for heavy users that is the right trade. See full specs and price.

5. Samsung Galaxy A07 — the trusted-brand pick (Rs 47,999)

Some buyers simply want the Samsung name, service network and software support — and the Galaxy A07 is the way to get it under Rs 50,000 without giving up performance: it uses the same Helio G99 chipset as the Hot 40 Pro, with a 90Hz display, a 50 MP main camera and a 5000 mAh battery with 25W charging. Storage goes up to 256 GB with a dedicated microSD slot. See full specs and price.

6. Infinix Note 50s 5G — the future-proof pick (Rs 49,999)

The only 5G phone in this list, and the most powerful: the Dimensity 7300 Ultimate (4 nm) sits a clear class above the Helio chips, the AMOLED display runs at 144Hz, and the 5500 mAh battery charges at 45W — a full charge in about an hour. If you keep a phone for three years or more, spending the full budget here makes sense. See full specs and price.

Which one should you buy?

Best overall value: Tecno Spark 30 Pro. For gaming: Infinix Hot 40 Pro. For camera: Redmi 13. For battery life: Vivo Y28. For brand and support: Samsung Galaxy A07. For 5G and long-term speed: Infinix Note 50s 5G.
