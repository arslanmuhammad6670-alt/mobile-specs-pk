Best Smartphones Under $200 (2026): Eight Phones That Don't Feel Cheap

Published 2026-11-12 · Check Specs

Something shifted in the last two years. The under-$200 shelf — once the home of laggy screens and cameras you apologised for — now holds phones with AMOLED displays, fast charging and chipsets that handle real games. If your budget is $200 (roughly Rs 55,000 in Pakistan), you are not settling any more. You are shopping. Here is how we would spend it.

Start with the screen: Spark 30 Pro and Note 13

The Tecno Spark 30 Pro at $150 is the deal we keep coming back to. Its AMOLED panel runs at 120Hz and gets properly bright, which no LCD phone at this price can match for contrast. Spend a little more and the Redmi Note 13 at $192 answers with its own 120Hz AMOLED, a Snapdragon 685, a 108 MP camera plus an ultrawide, and 33W charging that hits 50% in about half an hour. It is the most complete phone here if you can stretch to the top of the budget.

Gamers and battery hunters

For games, the Infinix Hot 40 Pro ($163) pairs the Helio G99 with up to 12 GB of RAM — the smoothest budget gaming combo we track at this price. For battery, two phones stand apart: the Tecno Pova 6 Neo ($181) packs a 7,000 mAh cell behind a 120Hz screen, and the Vivo Y28 ($170) counters with 6,000 mAh but much faster 44W charging. Pova for days away from a socket; Y28 if a quick top-up fits your routine better.

The safe and the smart

The Samsung Galaxy A07 ($173) is for buyers who value the brand's service network and software — and with a Helio G99 inside, it is not even slow any more. The Redmi 13 ($163) is the camera pick, because its 108 MP sensor has real PDAF autofocus, the thing budget cameras usually fake. And if you keep phones for years, the Infinix Note 50s 5G ($181) is the future-proofing play: a 4 nm Dimensity 7300 chip, a 144Hz AMOLED and the only 5G radio on this page.

One warning before you check out

At this end of the market, the variant matters more than the model. Several of these phones ship in 4 GB and 8 GB RAM versions that look identical in photos. Always confirm the exact RAM and storage you are paying for, and in Pakistan, buy the PTA-approved version with warranty.
