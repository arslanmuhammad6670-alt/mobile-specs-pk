iPad mini OLED (2026) Price in Pakistan: What the New Screen and A20 Pro Chip Cost You

Published 2026-11-12 · Check Specs

The iPad mini has always been the iPad for people who find iPads too big — readers, travellers, pilots, students who take notes in cramped lecture halls. In 2026 it got the upgrade owners had been requesting for years: an OLED screen. If you have been searching for its price in Pakistan, here it is, along with everything else that changed.

Price in Pakistan

We list the iPad mini (2026) at an estimated Rs 192,999 in Pakistan (about $698 internationally). As with most Apple products here, that is an estimate — actual shop prices vary with stock, storage variant and whether the unit is the Wi-Fi or cellular model, and early stock usually carries a premium. Storage runs 128, 256 and 512 GB.

What is new: the screen and the chip

The display is now an 8.5-inch Super Retina XDR OLED — the same display family as Apple's Pro tablets — at 600 nits typical brightness and up to 1,200 nits in HDR. In practical terms, that means true blacks and far better contrast than the LCD panel of the previous model — a clear step up, particularly for reading and video in a dark room. Inside, the A20 Pro chip with 12 GB of RAM offers substantial performance headroom, which matters at this price because buyers tend to keep iPads for many years. The design stays delightfully small — 195.3 mm tall and just 255 g for the Wi-Fi model — and it even picks up an IP67 rating this generation.

The rest of the picture

Cameras are 12 MP front and back, the front one ultrawide for video calls. The battery is rated at 4,900 mAh with fast wired charging (about 50% in 30 minutes). Touch ID lives in the top button, the connector is USB-C, and the cellular model uses eSIM. Who should not buy it? Anyone who mainly watches video on a big screen at home (a larger, cheaper iPad serves you better) and anyone put off by estimated-price early stock — waiting a few weeks usually softens Pakistani launch prices.

Should you upgrade from the 2024 mini?

If your 2024 iPad mini still handles everything you ask of it, the only compelling reason to upgrade is the screen. For first-time mini buyers, this is the strongest version of the formula so far — just buy it from a shop that gives you a proper bill and warranty, because at Rs 192,999 you want paperwork.

Frequently asked questions

Is the iPad mini (2026) officially available in Pakistan?

Apple does not sell directly in Pakistan, so units reach the market through importers and authorised resellers. That is why the price here is listed as estimated: it varies with stock and variant, and early stock usually costs more.

Does the Wi-Fi model need PTA approval?

No — a Wi-Fi-only tablet does not connect to mobile networks and needs no registration. If you are considering the cellular model, check its DIRBS registration status before paying, since an unregistered device cannot use a Pakistani SIM or eSIM.

What is the difference from the 2024 iPad mini?

Based on the published specifications: an OLED display in place of LCD, the newer A20 Pro chip with 12 GB of RAM, an IP67 rating, and a slightly larger 8.5-inch screen. The full comparison is on the two models' specification pages.
