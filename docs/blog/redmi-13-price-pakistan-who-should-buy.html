Redmi 13 Price in Pakistan (Rs 44,999): Full Specs and Who Should Actually Buy It

Published 2026-11-12 · Check Specs

Xiaomi knows exactly what it is doing with the Redmi 13. The box leads with "108 MP", because that is the number that stops people in a shop. Fine — cameras matter. But a phone is more than its biggest number, so here is the whole picture for the Rs 44,999 the Redmi 13 costs in Pakistan, including the parts the marketing leaves out.

The specs that matter

Display6.79-inch IPS LCD, 90Hz
ChipsetMediaTek Helio G91 Ultra
RAM / Storage6 or 8 GB / 128 or 256 GB (microSD via shared SIM slot)
Main camera108 MP with PDAF + 2 MP macro
Selfie camera13 MP
Battery5,030 mAh, 33W charging
Price in PakistanRs 44,999

The camera: genuinely good, with one honest footnote

Here is the thing most listings will not tell you: megapixels are not what makes this camera good — autofocus is. The 108 MP sensor has PDAF, so it locks onto subjects quickly and accurately, and in daylight the detail is a clear step above the 50 MP cameras most rivals offer at this price. For photographing products for your shop, documents, or family events, it is the safest camera under Rs 50,000. The footnote: that 2 MP macro camera is decoration, and night photos are average. Judge it as a strong single camera, not a versatile system.

Where it compromises

Storage is eMMC 5.1, the slower standard, so installing big apps and copying files takes noticeably longer than on UFS phones. The screen is a 90Hz LCD — perfectly decent, but rivals offer 120Hz, and one (the Tecno Spark 30 Pro) gives you AMOLED for less money. And the Helio G91 Ultra is a daily-driver chip, not a gaming one; light games run well, heavy titles need lowered settings. None of these are deal-breakers at Rs 44,999. All of them are worth knowing before you are standing at the counter.

So who should buy it?

Camera-first buyers on a budget: yes, this is your phone. Parents buying for a student who photographs notes and everything else: also yes. Gamers: the Infinix Hot 40 Pro at the same price was built for you instead. Screen snobs: look at the Spark 30 Pro. The Redmi 13 does not try to be everything, and that honesty is exactly why it is easy to recommend to the right person.
